Connection colour key: Dupont on a 2.54 mm header, JST or other board socket, wire soldered to a pad. Battery and ESC power connections are not colour-coded and are judged separately.

BLUF

  • 30 iFlight flight controllers found: 25 sold on shop.iflight.com today (24 in stock, 1 out of stock) plus 5 discontinued boards that survive only as ArduPilot targets [1][2][5].
  • 11 have an ArduPilot target; only 6 of those are still sold: BLITZ Wing H743, BLITZ H7 Pro, Thunder H7, BLITZ F745, BLITZ Mini F745, BLITZ Whoop F7 AIO. All 11 have Copter 4.7.3 stable builds [5][6][7]. The whole newer Borg line (9 boards) and the Defender line have none; a Borg H7 pull request has been open since 2025-10 [8].
  • Best choice: BLITZ Wing H743, $76.49, in stock. The only iFlight board with any 2.54 mm holes and the only one with a separate signal output per ESC. Cost: 27 header pins soldered once; after that no wire touches a pad (3 needs on Dupont, 3 on JST-SH).
  • Runner-up: BLITZ H7 Pro, $81.59, in stock. All six needs come out on JST-GH sockets: zero soldering at the board, cable set in the box. Against it: zero Dupont, 35 x 35 mm mount (not 30.5), motors only on a 4-in-1-style 8-pin plug (fan-out harness needed), 4S minimum, board power arrives through that same plug.
  • Does any iFlight board need no soldering at all for the signal wiring? Yes, one: the BLITZ H7 Pro, at the board end, on sockets only. No iFlight board does it on Dupont. Thunder H7 misses by one soldered UART; the F745 pair by two soldered groups.
  • US stock for the six candidates: none found. DefianceRC lists four of them, all out of stock [10]. The maker's own store is the only confirmed in-stock source.

Row notes:

  • Wing H743: I2C is on JST-SH sockets and pads only, never on a 2.54 mm hole, so GPS + compass cannot move to Dupont [11][12]. Battery, 4x ESC power and the capacitor are solder pads on its power board.
  • H7 Pro: no battery pads on the FC; it is powered through the VBAT pin of the ESC plug, 14.8 V minimum (4S-12S) [3]. SERIAL5_PROTOCOL defaults to ESC telemetry on USART6 and must be changed for a MAVLink link [5]. Default frame type 12 (Betaflight X) [5]. Box: FC, cable set, USB adapter board [2]. A forum report ties a barometer failure to that USB adapter being left plugged in [12].
  • Thunder H7: no analog OSD. 1.5 A 5 V BEC is too small to share with a Pi. Input voltage: store text says 12S, ArduPilot README says 2S-6S: CONFLICT.
  • F745 / Mini F745: the GPS socket is UART4, but ArduPilot defaults GPS to UART3 (pads); using the socket needs SERIAL4_PROTOCOL 5 and has no DMA [5]. Both are powered through the ESC plug.
  • H7 targets: bidirectional DShot on outputs 1-8 (Wing, H7 Pro, Thunder). F745 targets: outputs 1-4 [5].
