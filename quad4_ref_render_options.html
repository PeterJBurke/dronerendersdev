4-inch reference quad: FC and companion options

Standalone model of the reference quad as the build photos show it; nose toward the orange arrow. V = verified from a design file or the maker's page; A = assumed or estimated from photos.
  1. Frame - CNC Drones MOD-L (Dave_C / ledroneclub, CC BY), 4" Arm v1-1 (3 mm) + HD_CARGO plates and cross plate (2 mm), 4x M2 x 25 standoffs drawn (the as-built drone has 6); 180.0 mm wheelbase, 147.2 x 103.6 mm stretched X (DXF, V).
  2. Matek F405-WMO FC + PDB, 32 x 22 x 12.7 mm, STM32F405 (mateksys.com, V); 8x3 and 5x3 blocks with right-angle headers and Dupont housings, arrow forward, VHB on the bottom plate; USB-C / buzzer extender at the tail (photos 167-186).
  3. Orange Pi Zero 3W - 65 x 32 x 1.2 mm (orangepi.org, V), flat on a riser above the top plate, long axis on the roll axis, ports right, 40-pin left, fan rear (photos 167-170); PCB drawn orange for contrast; heatsink case (opaque, 40-pin strip left open, case edge ASSUMED) hidden by default so the board shows; Pi case button shows it.
  4. Battery - Zeee 2S 2200 mAh (BOM) under the cross plate, drawn as a blue wrap with a white label band; strap not drawn.
  5. Motors, props, ESCs - XING2 1404 3800KV (gunmetal bell, teal top), Gemfan 4024 translucent blue (3 + 4 CW, 1 + 2 CCW), 20 A ESCs taped on the arms.
  6. Other - M100-PRO GPS on a tail mast, AKK A11T camera at the nose, EP2 RX rear-left, XIAO ESP32-C3 under the top plate.
  7. Options (panel, top right) - one flight controller and one companion computer at a time. As built: Matek F405-WMO and Orange Pi Zero 3W. The other boards are the 5-inch page's models placed on this frame. Lucid H7 Wing, Lucid H7 V2 + PDB and ARKV6X + PAB need the 5-inch quad bottom plate v2 (30.5 x 30.5 M3 press nuts, same HD_CARGO outline); Every option is drawn with four standoffs (nose and tail pairs; the as-built drone has six). Wing: right-angle headers only (25 mm stack). AeroEggTech AET-H743-Basic: stock plate, foam tape, four standoffs (no middle pair), board centred; not NDAA (PRC), battery / ESC power on solder pads. iFlight BLITZ Wing H743: plate v2, four standoffs (no middle pair), board centred, right-angle pins; 16.8 mm stack; not NDAA (PRC), battery / ESC power on solder pads. Cube Orange+ and Raspberry Pi 5 do not fit and are drawn to show why. Wiring and the USB-C extender are drawn for the Matek only. Option mounts are a proposal (ASSUMED).
Verified: frame outlines, holes, thicknesses (DXF/STL); FC envelope, MCU, pad names (Matek); Pi PCB size (Orange Pi); kit parts (BOM). Assumed: FC holes 18.1 x 12.0 mm and part positions (from Matek's layout image, +/-0.3 mm); Pi part positions, holes and riser; battery, ESC, GPS, RX sizes; wiring; the Pi FPC antenna (not in the photos).
