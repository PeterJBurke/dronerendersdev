BLUF

  • Everything fits. 5 UARTs needed (RC, GPS, 2 MAVLink, none for ESC RPM), 7 available; motors on S1-S4 with bidirectional DShot.
  • Recommended: 3 of 5 groups on Dupont (telemetry 1, telemetry 2, motors), 2 on the board's JST-SH sockets (receiver, GPS + compass). Most-Dupont alternative: 4 of 5, receiver on the centre hole column, no DMA (section 5).
  • GPS + compass cannot be on Dupont. I2C comes out only on JST-SH sockets and solder pads, never on a 2.54 mm hole [4].
  • Solder once: 27 header pins (the board ships with none [5]): 3 x 4 on the UART hole block, 3 x 4 on S1-S4, 1 x 3 on S12. Exception to the plug-in rule, not designed around: battery, 4x ESC power and the 470 uF capacitor are solder pads on the power board [4].
  • Main risk: the only UARTs on Dupont holes (6, 7, 8) have no DMA [2]. Keep both MAVLink links at 115200. Second risk: the Pi must not share the FC's 2.5 A 5 V BEC without a measured budget; feed it from the separate 6 A servo BEC.

4Rear right, CWS4 (PA1)S + GNDGroup 2, TIM5Yes / yes
  • Centre pin (silk BEC, VX on older drawings) is the 6 A servo rail. Do not connect it to an ESC: leave the centre cavity of each 1 x 3 housing empty.
  • S5 and S6 share TIM5 with S3 and S4: anything put there later must also be DShot [3].
  • RPM comes back on the signal wire. No ESC telemetry UART is used, which frees UART8 for the Pi.

Baud: UART6 and UART8 have no DMA and no RTS/CTS, so 115200 on both MAVLink links; do not raise it without a bench test for dropped bytes. Pi side: use the PL011 UART on GPIO14/15 (enable_uart=1, dtoverlay=disable-bt), serial console off.

Recommend Option B. The RC link is the one link that should have DMA and zero parameter changes; a JST-SH-to-Dupont cable still gives a Dupont end at the receiver. Use Option A only if a JST-SH 4-pin cable cannot be had, and bench-check RC frame rate and link statistics first.
