5-inch quad: Lucid H7 Wing

Frame + flight controller + motors + props only. No battery, camera or VTX. ESCs on the arms; flight controller and companion computer on the right (Options); penny for size.
  1. Frame - MOD-L 5-inch (Dave_C / ledroneclub, CC BY), 5" Arm v2-0 + HD_CARGO plates, mm wheelbase, stretched X; carbon, 4 mm arms, 2 mm plates, 6x M2 x 45 mm standoffs (kit: 25 mm); outlines from the designer's DXF files. 5-inch quad bottom plate v2 (30.5 x 30.5 M3 press nuts), design/cad/modl5_quad5/; 45 mm standoffs.
  2. Flight controller - Triumph Lucid H7 Wing NDAA (RMRC 87225): FC + PDB with current sensor, 54 x 36 x 13 mm, 25 g, 84 x 2.54 mm through-holes with headers; on M3 rubber grommets on the plate v2 press nuts. Right-angle headers, except straight pins with upright Dupont housings on F1 / F2 (housing top 34.0 mm, wire top 43.5 mm, 1.5 mm under the 45 mm top plate). Measure the Wing holes (manual suggests 28-29 mm). NDAA: GREEN.
  3. Motors - 4x 2004-size motor (T-Motor F2004 3000KV reference: 24.3 x 17.25 mm, 1.5 mm shaft, T-mount) on the stock 12 mm M2 pad of the 5" arm, 2 of 4 holes align (2 M2 screws per motor drawn); alternative UM2207 1860KV
  4. Propellers - 4x HQProp T5x2x3 T-mount tri-blade, 127 mm, 2.0 in pitch, blue; 2 CW + 2 CCW (pitch-correct for both hands)
  5. ESCs - 4x Lumenier Razor Pro F4 45A AM32 singles, 12 x 28 x 5 mm, 3 g, on the arms
  6. Companion computer - one at a time on the top plate, chosen in the Options panel on the right: Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W (default, 65 x 30 mm, velcro pad), Raspberry Pi 5 (85 x 56 mm, crossbars), Arduino UNO Q (68.6 x 53.4 mm, green, crossbars) or none. Mounts: README.
  7. GPS, radios - Triumph M9N GPS + compass (27 x 25 x 9 mm) on a 45 mm printed mast; RFD900ux-US (25 x 33 x 10.4 mm, 10 g) on a printed tray on the top-plate tail (antennas not drawn); Ewing EA2400RX (12 x 16.5 x 4.5 mm) on the rear-left standoff, 2.4 GHz T antenna. Mount positions ASSUMED (README).
  8. Dupont leads - 26 AWG; black housings on right-angle Wing headers, straight pins with upright housings on F1 / F2 (wiring report): blue TX, green RX, black GND, red 5 V, purple I2C, yellow DShot; beige JST at the parts. Wires on/off (#nowires).
Scale: 1 scene unit = 1 mm. Grid: 10 mm fine, 50 mm bold. Arrow on grid = nose. Scale bar: inches (1 in = 25.4 mm). The camera is perspective: the bar is exact on the grid; in the top view parts at motor / prop height read about 3-7 percent larger. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, right-drag or shift-drag to pan.
