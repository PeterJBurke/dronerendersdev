Flight controllers, to scale

The six flight controllers in designer's FlightControllers.docx, two Lumenier NDAA options and two Raspberry Pi boards for scale, flat on a 10 / 50 mm grid, 1 scene unit = 1 mm. Front row: FPV-size boards; back row: Wing and carriers; Pis at the right. NDAA: GREEN / RED per variants/README.md.
  1. Matek F405-WMO (4-inch reference quad board). 32 x 22 x 12.7 mm, 39 x 2.54 mm through-holes VERIFIED; 18 x 12 mm M2 mount MEASURED; separate USB-C / buzzer board not shown. RED (PRC).
  2. Lumenier LUX Mini F7 V2. 28 x 28 x 6 mm, 20 x 20 mm on M3 grommets VERIFIED; pads and wire holes only, no headers; pad positions MEASURED from photos. RED (China; not on the ArduPilot table, GetFPV "NDAA No").
  3. Triumph Lucid H7 (V2): 12 x 2.54 mm through-holes (Triumph NDAA build of the TBS Lucid H7 V2, RMRC 86342). 30.5 x 30.5 mm M3; one 1 x 10 edge column (SCL1, SDA1, G, 4V5, R2, T2, G, 4V5, R6, T6) + G / VBat pair, rest pads VERIFIED (TBS manual rev 1.15 p.38-40; the former 10 front + 9 rear model was V1); 36 x 36 outline PARTLY ASSUMED (RMRC vs manual 32 x 32); underside parts ASSUMED. GREEN (US-built Triumph SKU; TBS build is RED).
  4. Triumph Lucid H7 Wing NDAA (TBS Lucid H7 Wing design). 54 x 36 x 13 mm, 84 through-holes VERIFIED (TBS manual p.48 / p.51). GREEN (US-built Triumph SKU; TBS build is RED).
  5. CubePilot Cube Orange+ on the standard carrier. 94.5 x 44.3 mm, 31.4 mm tall, 85 x 34.4 mm M2.5 holes, connector zones VERIFIED (CubePilot drawing); 3 x 15 servo rail out the end face. GREEN (Blue UAS).
  6. ARK ARKV6X on the ARK PAB carrier. Module 36 x 29 x 5, carrier 74 x 43.5 x 12 mm VERIFIED; connector positions and ~68 x 39 mm holes ASSUMED (photo). JST-GH only. GREEN (Blue UAS).
  7. Lumenier LUX F765 NDAA (added NDAA option). 38 x 38 x 4 mm, 9 g, 30.5 mm M3 grommets VERIFIED (GetFPV); JST-SH ports + GH CAN, pads, no 2.54 mm (ArduPilot wiki); positions MEASURED by eye. GREEN (vendor declaration, made in Thailand). ArduPilot supported.
  8. Lumenier LUX H743 NDAA (added NDAA option). 37.5 x 37.5 mm, 30.5 mm M3 VERIFIED (PR #34831 README); JST-GH only, pads, no 2.54 mm; thickness and weight not published, 5 mm stack ASSUMED. GREEN (vendor claim, made in USA; not on Blue list). ArduPilot: PR #34831 open, vendor 4.7.2 build.
  9. Raspberry Pi 5 (right, back; ASSUMED model for "a Raspberry Pi", Pi 4B has the same 85 x 56 mm board and 58 x 49 mm M2.5 holes), about 46 g, 2 x 20 header fitted. Companion-computer size reference, not an FC.
  10. Raspberry Pi Zero W (right, front). 65 x 30 mm, 58 x 23 mm M2.5, about 9 g; 40-pin footprint shown unpopulated. Size reference.
The document pairs the Lucid H7 with a JHEMCU 45A 4-in-1 ESC; omitted because 5-inch quad uses discrete ESCs.
2.54 mm headers (black base 2.5 mm, 0.64 mm pins 6 mm proud) only where the board has 2.54 mm through-holes: WMO, Lucid H7 V2 (12), H7 Wing, Cube servo rail, Pi 5 (Pi Zero W holes left empty). FC boards drawn green for consistency (Matek blue, Cube orange); tell the boards apart by the labels. Scale bar 6 in, 1 in ticks.
