4-inch reference quad

Standalone model of the reference quad as the build photos show it; nose toward the orange arrow. V = verified from a design file or the maker's page; A = assumed or estimated from photos.
  1. Frame - CNC Drones MOD-L (Dave_C / ledroneclub, CC BY), 4" Arm v1-1 (3 mm) + HD_CARGO plates and cross plate (2 mm), 6x M2 x 25 standoffs; 180.0 mm wheelbase, 147.2 x 103.6 mm stretched X (DXF, V).
  2. Matek F405-WMO FC + PDB, 32 x 22 x 12.7 mm, STM32F405 (mateksys.com, V); 8x3 and 5x3 blocks with right-angle headers and Dupont housings, arrow forward, VHB on the bottom plate; USB-C / buzzer extender at the tail (photos 167-186).
  3. Orange Pi Zero 3W - 65 x 32 x 1.2 mm (orangepi.org, V), flat on a riser above the top plate, long axis on the roll axis, ports right, 40-pin left, fan rear (photos 167-170); PCB drawn orange for contrast; heatsink case (opaque, 40-pin strip left open, case edge ASSUMED) hidden by default so the board shows; Pi case button shows it.
  4. Battery - Zeee 2S 2200 mAh (BOM) under the cross plate, drawn as a blue wrap with a white label band; strap not drawn.
  5. Motors, props, ESCs - XING2 1404 3800KV (gunmetal bell, teal top), Gemfan 4024 translucent blue (3 + 4 CW, 1 + 2 CCW), 20 A ESCs taped on the arms.
  6. Other - M100-PRO GPS on a tail mast, AKK A11T camera at the nose, EP2 RX rear-left, XIAO ESP32-C3 under the top plate.
