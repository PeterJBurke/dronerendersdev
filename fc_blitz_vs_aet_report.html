BLUF

  • Neither board can go on the NDAA BOM. Both are designed and made in the PRC (iFlight; Suzhou AeroEggTech). Section 848 covers flight controllers. Neither is on the Blue UAS list [19].
  • Better non-NDAA bench / reference board: BLITZ Wing H743. In stock at $76.49 [6], runs stock ArduPilot 4.7.3 [13], bidirectional DShot on outputs 1-8 [3], 10 of the 12 build connections plug in (6 on JST-SH sockets), 30.5 mm mount, 35 g.
  • AET-H743-Basic has two killers. (1) No bidirectional DShot in ArduPilot: its hwdef has no BIDIR outputs in 4.6.3, 4.7.3 or master [5]. (2) The current hardware V2.0 (BMI088 + BMI270) does not run the stock ArduPilot target; support is an open pull request [12][17]. Price and stock are still not confirmed.
  • Both fail the no-solder rule the same way the chosen Lucid H7 Wing does: battery and ESC power are solder pads. The only plug-in power path found is an optional board for the AET V2.0 [9].
  • Track record: iFlight (Huizhou, since 2014) is an established FPV maker with six boards in ArduPilot, the first in 2020; AeroEggTech (Suzhou) is small and recent, first seen in ArduPilot in late 2024, founding date not confirmed. No independent reviews found for either board.
  • Stack height: iFlight publishes the BLITZ stack height, 16.8 mm [7]. The 4-inch render uses it (corrected 2026-11-25 from a 13.8 mm estimate); both boards fit the 25 mm stack with about 1 mm or less to spare.

12. Recommendation

  1. Do not put either board on the NDAA BOM. Both are PRC flight controllers. Keep the Triumph Lucid H7 Wing.
  2. If a non-NDAA bench / reference board is wanted, buy the BLITZ Wing H743. Known price and stock, stock ArduPilot 4.7.3, bidirectional DShot on the four motor outputs, 30.5 mm mount, and GPS, receiver, camera/VTX, buzzer and USB on JST plugs.
  3. Do not buy the AET-H743-Basic for this build. No bidirectional DShot, a hardware revision that stock ArduPilot does not run, no confirmed source, no frame mount, 10 g heavier. Its better points (dual IMU, all-DMA UARTs, 4 A 5 V BEC, 10 A servo BEC, second battery monitor, RSSI input) do not offset those.
  4. Before ordering a BLITZ, budget for: a JST-SH 4-pin USB-C breakout (first flash), 2.54 mm headers, JST-SH 4/6/9-pin pigtails, a passive piezo, a 50 V 470 uF capacitor. Wire the camera to VI2 if VI1 shows grey.
  5. Worth one email to AeroEggTech: the V2.0 optional solder-free power board is the only plug-in battery / ESC power path seen on any of the three boards. Ask for connector type, current rating and whether it is sold separately. It does not change the NDAA answer.
